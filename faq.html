FAQ

Is Grindly free?

Yes. One habit is free forever. Download it on the App Store and start.

What does Pro add?

Unlimited habits, widgets, playing jokers, and data export. It is a one-time purchase: $3.99 US / 3,99 EUR in Slovenia. Nothing renews.

Do you have a subscription?

No.

Where does my data live?

On your device. If iCloud is on, in your personal iCloud account. Grindly has no server of its own. Details: Privacy Policy.

What are jokers?

A 7-day streak mints one (max two). Play one and yesterday turns gold. The miss stays visible. How jokers work.

Which devices?

Yes. iOS on iPhone and iPad. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. No Android.

How do I get support?

Open a GitHub issue.
