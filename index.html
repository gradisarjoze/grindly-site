Grindly

Your grind, visualized.

Grindly tracks habits on a grid of colored tiles. Show up, tap once, and today fills in. Skip a day and the gap stays on the record. The grid doesn't flatter you, which is exactly why a full month of it feels so good.

The grid is the app.

Each habit gets its own grid. Tap the checkmark and today fills in. The card shows your streak, your best run, and your completion rate. Widgets put the checkmark on your home screen, and when the last habit of the day is done, Grindly throws a small party.
