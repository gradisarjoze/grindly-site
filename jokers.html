Jokers

A joker is insurance, not a cheat. It keeps the streak alive without painting a missed day as done. Other apps call this a streak freeze. A Grindly joker still leaves the miss on the grid.

How you get one

Every 7-day streak mints a joker. You can hold two. Minting is free.

How you play one

Playing a joker is a Pro feature. Yesterday's tile turns gold. The streak holds. The gap stays on the grid so the month still tells the truth.
