Grindly

Privacy Policy

Effective August 24, 2026

Grindly is built around a simple principle: your data is yours. It lives on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your personal iCloud account. It never touches a server the developer can read.

What we collect

Nothing. Grindly has no accounts, no sign-up, and no servers of its own. The habits you create and the completions you record are stored on your device. The developer never sees, receives, or collects any of it.

What stays on your device

  • Habits and completions live in the app's local database, shared only with Grindly's own home-screen widgets on your device.
  • Notifications (reminders, nudges, and celebrations) are local notifications, scheduled and delivered entirely on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Settings such as theme and notification preferences are stored on your device.

iCloud sync

Grindly syncs your habits between your devices through your personal iCloud account, using your private CloudKit database. That data belongs to your Apple account: it counts toward your own iCloud storage (habit data amounts to a few kilobytes), it is encrypted in transit, and the developer has no way to access it. Sync happens automatically when you are signed into iCloud; you can turn it off for Grindly in iOS Settings under your Apple account's iCloud storage options.

Purchases

Grindly Pro is a one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we receive no personal or payment information about you. Purchases can be restored through your Apple account. Apple's handling of your data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

There are none. Grindly contains no analytics frameworks, no advertising, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

Data export and deletion

You can export your data at any time from Settings → Export Data. The export is created on your device and shared only where you choose to send it. Deleting a habit permanently removes it and its history, including the synced copy in your iCloud. Deleting the app removes all Grindly data from your device; synced data can be removed from iCloud in iOS Settings.

Children

Grindly does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If Grindly's data practices ever change, this page will be updated and the effective date revised before the change ships.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
